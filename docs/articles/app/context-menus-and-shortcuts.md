This is one of the app's own features. It is marked **In progress**, which means the description below is the plan, not something you can use yet.

> [!NOTE]
> Context menus and keyboard shortcuts is **In progress**. Treat everything under "Planned behaviour" as a goal until this page says otherwise.

## What it is for

Get the actions that apply to a device, rule or log line right where you are looking at it.

## Planned behaviour

- Right-click or long-press shows actions for that item, such as rename, block or copy.
- Every menu action is also available as a visible button.
- A shortcut list shows every key combination.
- Shortcuts can be changed, and conflicts are explained.

## What is missing

Context menus and the shortcut editor are not built.

## Good to know

- On a phone, a long press is the equivalent of a right-click.
- Menus never hide an action that has no other route.
