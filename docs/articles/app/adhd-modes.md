This is one of the app's own features. It is marked **In progress**, which means the description below is the plan, not something you can use yet.

> [!NOTE]
> Focus and ADHD modes is **In progress**. Treat everything under "Planned behaviour" as a goal until this page says otherwise.

## What it is for

Reduce visual noise and break long tasks into small steps, for people who find busy screens hard to work with.

## Planned behaviour

- Focus layout shows one task at a time with a clear next step.
- Optional gentle reminders for steps you started and did not finish.
- Fewer simultaneous animations and no flashing.
- A simple timer for a task, with no pressure wording.

## What is missing

No focus layout, reminders or timer exist yet. The design is not final.

## Good to know

- Modes are options, not diagnoses. Anyone can turn them on.
- Reduced motion is always honoured.
