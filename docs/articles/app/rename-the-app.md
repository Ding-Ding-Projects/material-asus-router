This is one of the app's own features. It is marked **In progress**, which means the description below is the plan, not something you can use yet.

> [!NOTE]
> Rename the app is **In progress**. Treat everything under "Planned behaviour" as a goal until this page says otherwise.

## What it is for

Call your installation "Home router" or anything else you prefer, so it is easy to recognise among your browser tabs.

## Planned behaviour

- The owner types a new name in Settings and sees it in the header and tab title straight away.
- A reset button restores the original name.
- The documentation and window titles keep the product name.
- Names have a length limit and are shown as plain text only.

## What is missing

The setting and the places that read it are not built.

## Good to know

- Renaming changes display text only, not the address or the data.
- Only the owner can rename the app.
