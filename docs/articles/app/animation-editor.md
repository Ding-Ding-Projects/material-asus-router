This is one of the app's own features. It is marked **In progress**, which means the description below is the plan, not something you can use yet.

> [!NOTE]
> Animation and transition editor is **In progress**. Treat everything under "Planned behaviour" as a goal until this page says otherwise.

## What it is for

See and adjust every kind of motion in the app, from page changes to progress bars, with a live preview of each.

## Planned behaviour

- A list of animated elements, each with on or off, duration and easing.
- A global speed control and a master switch.
- Ready-made profiles such as Calm and Standard, plus your own saved profile.
- Import and export a profile as JSON.

## What is missing

The editor and the motion system it controls are not built.

## Good to know

- When your device asks for reduced motion, motion stays off whatever the editor says.
- The guard countdown stays readable with motion off.
