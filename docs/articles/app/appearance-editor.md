This is one of the app's own features. It is marked **In progress**, which means the description below is the plan, not something you can use yet.

> [!NOTE]
> Appearance editor is **In progress**. Treat everything under "Planned behaviour" as a goal until this page says otherwise.

## What it is for

Tune how the app looks so it is comfortable to read: light or dark, accent colour, spacing and font size.

## Planned behaviour

- Light, dark and follow-the-device themes.
- Pick an accent colour and see the whole app update live.
- Compact, comfortable and spacious density.
- High-contrast and larger-text options.

## What is missing

The editor screen and the settings that store the choices are not built.

## Good to know

- All colour choices are checked for readable contrast.
- Settings are stored per person.
