This is one of the app's own features. It is marked **In progress**, which means the description below is the plan, not something you can use yet.

> [!NOTE]
> Support tickets is **In progress**. Treat everything under "Planned behaviour" as a goal until this page says otherwise.

## What it is for

Record a problem with the app, with the app version, firmware family and recent history attached, so it is easy to explain or report.

## Planned behaviour

- A form that fills in version and firmware information for you.
- A choice of what to attach, with secret values always removed.
- A list of your tickets and their state.
- Export a ticket as text to send to whoever helps you.

## What is missing

The form, the attachment rules and the ticket list are not built. There is no hosted support service behind it.

## Good to know

- Tickets stay on your installation unless you export them.
- Nothing is sent anywhere automatically.

## A planned walkthrough (demonstration)

Wi-Fi drops every evening. Instead of describing it from memory:

1. Open **New ticket** and give it a short title.
2. The app offers to attach the details that help: model, firmware version, the last changes from the [change history](../safety/change-history.md) and a trimmed log.
3. You review everything it will attach. Passwords, keys and addresses you mark private are removed.
4. The ticket is saved in the app. You can copy it into a message, or keep it as a record.

## What is never attached

Secrets, vault contents and anything you did not tick.

## Questions people ask

**Is anything sent automatically?** No. Nothing leaves the app unless you copy and send it yourself.
