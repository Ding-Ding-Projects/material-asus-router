This is one of the app's own features. It is marked **In progress**, which means the description below is the plan, not something you can use yet.

> [!NOTE]
> Drawing is **In progress**. Treat everything under "Planned behaviour" as a goal until this page says otherwise.

## What it is for

Sketch where the router, the access points and the main devices sit, or jot a quick diagram to share with someone helping you.

## Planned behaviour

- Pen, shapes, text and an eraser.
- Undo and redo while you draw.
- Export the drawing as an image.
- Drawings stay on your device unless you export them.

## What is missing

The canvas and its tools are not built.

## Good to know

- Drawings are not connected to router data.
- School mode hides this feature.
