This is one of the app's own features. It is marked **In progress**, which means the description below is the plan, not something you can use yet.

> [!NOTE]
> Narrator is **In progress**. Treat everything under "Planned behaviour" as a goal until this page says otherwise.

## What it is for

Have the app read out previews, results and warnings using your device's speech voices, in addition to normal screen reader support.

## Planned behaviour

- Reads the change preview, the guard countdown and the result aloud.
- Picks a voice matching the app language.
- Speed, volume and what is narrated can be changed.
- Never reads secret values aloud.

## What is missing

No narration code yet. Voice quality depends on the voices installed on your device.

## Good to know

- This supplements, and does not replace, screen reader support.
- Narration is off by default.
