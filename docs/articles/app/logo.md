This is one of the app's own features. It is marked **In progress**, which means the description below is the plan, not something you can use yet.

> [!NOTE]
> Logo is **In progress**. Treat everything under "Planned behaviour" as a goal until this page says otherwise.

## What it is for

Choose the small mark shown next to the app name. The default is the product mark.

## Planned behaviour

- Upload a small image, which is checked and resized on your device.
- Choose from a few built-in marks.
- A reset button returns to the default mark.
- The logo is used for the browser tab icon where the browser allows it.

## What is missing

Upload, checks and the places that show the logo are not built.

## Good to know

- Only plain images are accepted, with a size limit.
- Only the owner can change the logo.
