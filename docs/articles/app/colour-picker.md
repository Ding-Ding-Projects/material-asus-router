This is one of the app's own features. It is marked **In progress**, which means the description below is the plan, not something you can use yet.

> [!NOTE]
> Colour picker is **In progress**. Treat everything under "Planned behaviour" as a goal until this page says otherwise.

## What it is for

Choose an accent colour by sliding, typing a value or picking a swatch, and see at once whether text on it is readable.

## Planned behaviour

- Hue, saturation and lightness controls plus typed hex values.
- A contrast readout for text on the chosen colour.
- Recent and suggested colours.
- Works fully by keyboard with labelled controls.

## What is missing

The picker control is not built.

## Good to know

- A colour that fails the contrast check is flagged rather than silently accepted.
- Colour is never the only way the app shows a state.
