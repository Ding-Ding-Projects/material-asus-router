This is one of the app's own features. It is marked **In progress**, which means the description below is the plan, not something you can use yet.

> [!NOTE]
> Action sounds is **In progress**. Treat everything under "Planned behaviour" as a goal until this page says otherwise.

## What it is for

Hear a short cue when a change is applied, confirmed or fails, with a clear switch to turn them off.

## Planned behaviour

- Distinct sounds for applied, undone, guard fired and error.
- A visible switch in Settings and a volume control.
- Sounds never replace the visible result or the screen reader announcement.
- Quiet if your device is muted or school mode is on.

## What is missing

No sound set and no settings control yet.

## Good to know

- Sounds are short and never repeat quickly.
- The switch persists after you restart.
