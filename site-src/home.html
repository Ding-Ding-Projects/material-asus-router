How a safe change works一個安全改動點樣運作

Scroll through the five moments between pressing Apply and a router you can still reach. The picture on the side shows a demonstration with made-up values.向下捲，睇睇由撳「套用」到仲搵得返路由器之間嘅五個時刻。側邊嘅圖係用虛構數值做嘅示範。

Demonstration · made-up values示範 · 虛構數值

App程式 Router路由器198.51.100.1
Preview預覽HomeNet HomeNet-Fast
Saved copy備份副本HomeNet kept for undo留低用嚟還原
90 srevert timer還原計時器

Reached: timer cancelled連到：計時器取消Lost: router restores itself連唔到：路由器自動還原

  1. 1

    Preview預覽

    You see the old and the new value side by side in plain words. Nothing has changed yet, and you can walk away.你會用日常說話睇到新舊數值並排。呢一步仲未改任何嘢，隨時可以走人。

  2. 2

    Save a copy留一份副本

    Before anything is written, the app keeps the current values. That copy is what makes one-click undo possible.寫入之前，程式先記低而家嘅數值。有咗呢份副本，先可以一鍵還原。

  3. 3

    Arm the timer啟動計時器

    For changes that can affect the network, the router is told to put the old values back in 90 seconds unless you confirm.如果改動可能影響網絡，路由器會收到指示：90 秒內冇人確認，就自動還原舊數值。

  4. 4

    Apply套用

    The change is written. If it cuts your link, that is fine: the timer on the router is already running by itself.改動寫入路由器。就算斷咗線都唔使驚，路由器上面嘅計時器已經自己行緊。

  5. 5

    Check the link檢查連線

    If the app reaches the router again, it cancels the timer and records the change. If not, the router restores the saved values and your connection comes back.程式再連到路由器，就會取消計時器同記錄呢次改動。連唔到，路由器自己還原備份，你嘅連線就返嚟。
